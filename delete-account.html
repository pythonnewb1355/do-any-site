Delete your Do Any account

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and data for the app Do Any (com.remindly.orbit), published by RemindlyStudios.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to g00419982@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Do Any account".
  2. Send it from the same Google account email you used to sign in to the app, so we can verify it is you.

What is deleted

  • Your account record (name, email address, Google account ID)
  • All reminders synced to our cloud database

All of the above is deleted permanently within 30 days of your request. No data is retained after deletion.

Reminders stored only on your device are removed when you uninstall the app.

Questions

Email g00419982@gmail.com.
