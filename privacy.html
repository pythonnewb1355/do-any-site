Do Any — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Do Any ("the app") is a reminder app published by RemindlyStudios. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and Google account ID. This is used only to create and identify your account.
  • Your reminders. The reminders you create (title, date and time, status) are stored on your device and synced to our cloud database (Google Firebase) so they are backed up and available if you reinstall the app.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not show ads and do not use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or files.

How your data is protected

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored in Google Firebase, which encrypts data at rest.

Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. See our account deletion page, or email us at g00419982@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with. We will delete your account and all reminders within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "last updated" date above.
